Privacy Policy

Game: The Mad Inspector
Effective date: October 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how The Mad Inspector (“the Game”), developed and published by KABOOM Game Developers, processes user data when the Game is used on Meta Quest / Meta Horizon.

1. Data We Process

The Game uses certain Meta Horizon Platform features. As a result, the Game may process the following data provided by Meta:

  • Meta app-scoped User ID: a unique identifier used to identify the current player within The Mad Inspector.
  • In-App Purchase (IAP) and entitlement information: information provided by Meta about purchases and entitlements, such as the product/SKU purchased, purchase or entitlement status, and other transaction information Meta makes available to the Game for purchase verification.

The Game may also use local save data and settings stored on the user’s device, such as game progress, preferences, and in-game currency balances.

We do not collect Meta account passwords, payment card numbers, bank account details, or other payment credentials. Payment processing is handled by Meta.

2. How We Use This Data

We process the data above only as necessary to operate the Game and its purchase features, including to:

  • Identify the current player across game sessions using the Meta app-scoped User ID.
  • Associate Meta purchase entitlements with the correct player.
  • Verify successful purchases of consumable in-game cash packs.
  • Grant the correct amount of in-game currency after a successful purchase.
  • Reconcile purchase status when needed and prevent duplicate or incorrect grants.
  • Consume eligible consumable entitlements after delivery so the product can be purchased again.
  • Provide support, investigate purchase problems, and maintain the security and integrity of the Game.

3. Advertising, Profiling, and Sale of Data

We do not use the Meta app-scoped User ID or IAP data for targeted advertising, behavioral profiling, or marketing, and we do not sell this data to third parties.

4. Data Sharing and Service Providers

At this time, KABOOM Game Developers does not use a separate third-party data processor or cloud service provider to process Meta Horizon User Data on our behalf. Meta processes platform and payment data under Meta’s own terms and privacy policies.

We may disclose information if required by applicable law or a valid legal process.

5. Data Storage and Retention

We only retain user data for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including purchase verification, support, fraud prevention, and legal obligations.

At present, we do not operate a separate external user database that permanently stores Meta app-scoped User IDs or Meta purchase records outside the Meta platform. The Game may process this information during operation and purchase verification. Local save data may remain on the user’s device until it is deleted by the user, reset, or removed with the Game, depending on the device and platform behavior.

6. Data Deletion Requests

Any user, regardless of location, may request deletion of user data that we have collected or stored in connection with The Mad Inspector.

To request deletion, email themadinspector715@gmail.com with the subject line “Data Deletion Request - The Mad Inspector”.

Please provide enough information for us to reasonably identify the relevant Game account or purchase record. Do not send passwords, payment card numbers, or other sensitive credentials. If we hold data associated with the request, we will delete it without undue delay unless retention is required by law or necessary to resolve a transaction, fraud, security, or legal issue. If no externally stored user data is held by us, we will explain that in our response.

7. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards appropriate to the data we process. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

8. Children

The Mad Inspector is intended for teens and adults (13+ or the applicable minimum age in the user’s region). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children below the applicable minimum age.

9. Third-Party Platform

The Game is distributed through Meta Quest / Meta Horizon. Meta may independently collect and process information under Meta’s own privacy policy and terms. This Privacy Policy describes KABOOM Game Developers’ handling of data made available to the Game through Meta platform features.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the Game, its platform features, or our data practices change. The updated policy will be posted at this same public URL with a revised effective date.
